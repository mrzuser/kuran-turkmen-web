Privacy Policy for Kuran Türkmen

Effective Date: September 2026

Thank you for choosing to use Kuran Türkmen ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about our policy, or our practices with regards to your personal information, please contact the developer via the email provided on our Google Play Store listing.

1. Information We Collect

Our application focuses on providing Islamic resources, including the Holy Quran (in Turkmen), prayer times, Hijri calendar, and Adhan (Azan) notifications. To provide these features, we request certain permissions on your device:

  • Location Data: We may request access to your device's location (approximate and/or precise) solely to calculate accurate prayer times (Namaz) and Qibla direction based on your specific geographical area. This data is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to, stored on, or shared with our servers or any third parties.
  • Notifications & Alarms: We request permissions to set alarms and send notifications to alert you when it is prayer time (Adhan/Azan) or for daily Islamic reminders.
  • Background Services: We use background services to play Quran recitations continuously even when the app is closed or the screen is locked.

2. How We Use Your Information

Any data accessed by the app (such as your location) is used strictly to deliver the app's core functionalities. We do not collect, store, sell, or monetize your personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app does not integrate with any third-party ad networks or data brokers. Audio files (such as Quran recitations) and text data are fetched from trusted Islamic sources directly to your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or regulatory requirements. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the developer email address listed on our Google Play Store page.
