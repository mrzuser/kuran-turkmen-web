Thank you for using Kuran Türkmen - Namaz & Azan ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are firmly committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, how device features are utilized, and clarifies that we do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your personal information to external servers.

Key Summary: Kuran Türkmen - Namaz & Azan is designed to be privacy-first. All Quran text, translations, prayer time calculations, and user settings are stored locally on your device. We do not use third-party advertising networks, profiling cookies, or analytics tracking SDKs.

1. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To provide accurate religious services such as prayer times, timely Azan (Adhan) alarms, and uninterrupted Quran audio recitation, the App requests specific permissions on your Android device:

📍 Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) Purpose: Used exclusively to calculate accurate local Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and to determine the Qibla direction based on your geographic coordinates.
Data Handling: Location calculation occurs entirely locally on your device. Your location coordinates are never transmitted to our servers, never logged, and never shared with any third party.
⏰ Exact Alarms & Reminders (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT) Purpose: Prayer times shift daily based on the sun's position. Exact alarm permission is required so the application can trigger Azan audio notifications precisely at the exact prayer minute, even when the device is in low-power or Doze mode.
Data Handling: Alarm schedules are purely handled by the Android operating system on your device.
🔊 Foreground Service - Media Playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) Purpose: To allow continuous listening to Holy Quran recitations and playing Azan audio notifications in the background while the screen is locked or when you navigate to other applications.
User Control: A persistent playback notification is displayed allowing you to pause, play, or dismiss audio playback at any time.
🔔 Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK) Purpose: To notify you when a prayer time enters and to deliver daily verses (Aýat), prayers (Doga), or Islamic reminders configured by you.
User Control: You have complete control to enable, disable, or customize notification sounds in the application settings or via Android system settings.
🔄 Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) Purpose: When your phone restarts, scheduled prayer alarms are automatically re-registered so you do not miss any prayer notifications.

2. Data Collection, Storage, and Sharing

  • No Personal Identifiers Collected: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or files.
  • No Tracking or Analytics SDKs: The App does not integrate third-party tracking libraries, analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics for user profiling), or advertising identifiers.
  • No Advertising Networks: The application does not contain advertisements (no AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.) and does not share device identifiers with ad networks.
  • Local Storage Only: All personal preferences (such as your chosen reciter, bookmark history, font sizes, prayer reminder offsets, and notes) are saved exclusively in your device’s local storage (SQLite / Async Storage).

3. Third-Party Audio Repositories

Quran recitations by renowned reciters (Qaris) are streamed or downloaded directly from trusted, public Quran CDN servers (such as EveryAyah / Quran Central repositories). These network requests only fetch the selected audio files and do not transmit any identifiable personal data.

4. External Links & Interactive Services (Telegram & Third Parties)

The Application may include optional external links or redirects to third-party platforms, such as an external Telegram bot (e.g., for community communication, spiritual advice, or Abjad/Ebced calculation):

  • Voluntary Interaction: Clicking any external Telegram link opens the Telegram app or website upon your explicit action. The App itself does not collect or log your Telegram username, messages, or activity.
  • No Data Export: The App does not automatically send any of your device information, local bookmarks, or location coordinates to Telegram or any bot administrator.
  • Third-Party Policies: Any information you voluntarily choose to share inside Telegram is governed entirely by Telegram’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

5. Age Restrictions (18+ Policy)

Our application is rated and intended for an adult audience (18+ years of age). We do not knowingly market to, target, or collect any personal information from children. If you believe a minor has accessed the application or any external linked services, please note that no personal data is collected or stored on our servers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect or transmit your data to any remote database, we do not maintain personal user accounts or store any personal data on external servers. You can permanently erase all local application data, bookmarks, and settings at any time by:

  • Clearing app storage via Android Settings → Apps → Kuran Türkmen → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the application from your device.

7. Security of Your Experience

We prioritize user trust and application safety. All bundled database assets (Quran translations, Hadiths, Islamic calendar) are cryptographically validated, and network connections for Quran audio retrieval use industry-standard HTTPS encryption.
