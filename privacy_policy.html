1. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To provide accurate religious services such as local prayer times, Qibla compass direction, timely Azan (Adhan) alarms, and uninterrupted Quran audio recitation, the App requests specific permissions on your Android device:

📍 Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) Purpose: Used exclusively to calculate accurate local Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and to determine the Qibla compass direction based on your geographic coordinates.
Data Safety & Local Processing: Location data is processed entirely locally on your device. Your geographic coordinates are never transmitted to any remote server, never logged, and never shared with any third party.
🔋 Battery Optimization Exemption & Exact Alarms (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT) Purpose (EN): Used exclusively so that prayer time and Azan alarms are not delayed by the Android operating system's sleep mode (Doze Mode) and can ring at the exact prayer time. No personal data is collected in the background.
Açıklama (TR): Yalnızca namaz vakti ve ezan alarmlarının Android işletim sisteminin uyku modu (Doze Mode) tarafından geciktirilmemesi ve tam vaktinde çalabilmesi amacıyla kullanılır.
🔊 Foreground Service - Media Playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) Purpose: To allow continuous listening to Holy Quran recitations and playing Azan audio notifications in the background while the screen is locked or when you navigate to other applications.
User Control: A persistent playback notification is displayed allowing you to pause, play, or dismiss audio playback at any time.
🔔 Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK) Purpose: To notify you when a prayer time enters and to deliver daily verses (Aýat), prayers (Doga), or Islamic reminders configured by you.
User Control: You have complete control to enable, disable, or customize notification sounds in the application settings or via Android system settings.
🔄 Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) Purpose: When your phone restarts, scheduled prayer alarms are automatically re-registered locally on your device so you do not miss any prayer notifications.

2. Data Safety, Collection, Storage, and Sharing

  • No Personal Identifiers Collected: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal identifiers such as your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or files.
  • On-Device Location Processing Only: Location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) are used strictly on-device for prayer time and Qibla compass calculations. Location data is never sent to any remote server or shared with third parties.
  • No Tracking or Analytics SDKs: The App does not contain or integrate any third-party tracking libraries, analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), or user profiling tools.
  • No Advertising SDKs: The application is completely ad-free (no AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, etc.) and does not collect or share Advertising IDs.
  • Local Storage Only: All personal preferences (such as your chosen reciter, bookmark history, font sizes, prayer reminder offsets, and notes) are saved exclusively in your device’s local storage (SQLite / Async Storage).

3. Third-Party Audio Repositories & Open Data Sources

Quran recitations by renowned reciters (Qaris) are streamed or downloaded directly from trusted, public Quran CDN servers (EveryAyah.com and MP3Quran.net). Bundled offline religious content includes Quran data provided by Quran Foundation (quran.foundation), Turkmen translation by the Muftiate of Turkmenistan (via QUL / Tarteel AI), and Hadith data provided by HadeethEnc.com (hadeethenc.com). Network requests for audio files only fetch the selected recitation and do not transmit any identifiable personal data.
